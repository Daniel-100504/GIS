User Guide

A quick walkthrough for MENRO staff & field personnel

1

Dashboard

Open Dashboard from the menu for a summary of zone health, NDVI, and surveys.

2

Map

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, or use the +/− controls.

3

Search

Use the search box in the right panel to filter zones by barangay name.

4

Layers

Toggle Sentinel-2 imagery, NDVI overlay, and zone markers under Map Layers.

5

Selecting a Zone

Click a zone marker or list item to see its NDVI, health status, and field survey details.

6

Export Report

Choose Export Field Survey Report from the menu, then pick PDF or Excel to download all recorded field surveys.

7

Satellite NDVI

Picking a Scene Date automatically syncs each zone's satellite NDVI in the background — this can take a moment.

8

KoboToolbox

Field surveys sync automatically — a zone updates on its own once a ranger submits one.
