User Guide

A quick walkthrough for MENRO staff & field personnel

1

Dashboard

Open Dashboard from the menu for a system-wide summary — total zones, health breakdown, average NDVI and canopy cover, survey counts, and the most commonly reported threat.

2

Map

Scroll to zoom in and out. Click and drag to pan around Calatagan. Use the + / − controls in the top-left corner of the map for precise zoom steps.

3

Search

Use the search box under Mangrove Zones in the right panel to filter by barangay name. The list below updates instantly as you type.

4

Layers

In the right panel under Map Layers, toggle Satellite Base, Sentinel-2 Imagery, NDVI Overlay, and Mangrove Zones on or off to control what's shown on the map.

5

Selecting a Zone

Click a zone marker on the map, or a zone in the list, to open its detail popup — partner organization, NDVI, health status, and (if available) field survey data like ranger name, canopy cover, and observed threats.

6

Export Report

Choose Export Field Survey Report from the menu to generate a PDF summarizing the current scene — zone overview table plus full field survey details for surveyed zones.

7

Sync Satellite NDVI

Choose Sync Satellite NDVI from the menu to pull a real mean NDVI reading for every zone from Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery, based on the currently selected Scene Date. This runs zone-by-zone and can take a little while — it's shown alongside the field-survey NDVI in each zone's popup rather than replacing it.

8

KoboToolbox

Field surveys submitted through the KoboToolbox mobile form sync automatically. Once a ranger submits a survey for a barangay, that zone's NDVI, status, and detail panel update on their own — no manual entry needed here.
